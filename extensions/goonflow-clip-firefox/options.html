Sign-in cookies

Only f95, E-hentai and Exhentai need a login cookie, and the app keeps it inside the encrypted vault. This list is what the app recorded for each site - the extension keeps no copy, stores nothing in browser storage, and never shows the value. To hand one over: open the site while logged in, click the toolbar icon, and use Send this page's sign-in cookie.

f95 (f95) wants xf_session and xf_user not asked yet
E-hentai (ehentai) wants ipb_member_id and ipb_pass_hash not asked yet
Exhentai (exhentai) wants ipb_member_id and ipb_pass_hash not asked yet

Check asks the app what it has recorded and what its own probe concluded (GET /api/cookie) - it does not send a cookie and the app never answers with one. Whether a cookie really works is the app's call: it probes the site and reports which keys it found. No xf_session means you were logged out when it was taken.
